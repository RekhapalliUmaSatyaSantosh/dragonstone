House Targaryen

House Targaryen is one of the oldest and most legendary noble houses in the world of Game of Thrones and House of the Dragon. Descended from the mighty dragonlords of the ancient Freehold of Valyria, the Targaryens escaped the Doom of Valyria and settled on the volcanic island fortress of Dragonstone.


More than a century later, Aegon I Targaryen, known as Aegon the Conqueror, united the Seven Kingdoms of Westeros. Riding the legendary dragon Balerion the Black Dread, alongside his sisters Visenya and Rhaenys, he forged the Iron Throne from the swords of his defeated enemies and founded the Targaryen dynasty.


For nearly 300 years, House Targaryen ruled Westeros. Their reign was marked by powerful dragonriders, wise kings, fierce warriors, and devastating civil wars. The greatest conflict, known as the Dance of the Dragons, nearly destroyed both the dragons and the family itself.


The dynasty finally fell during Robert's Rebellion, when the Mad King, Aerys II Targaryen, was overthrown. The surviving heirs fled across the Narrow Sea, keeping alive the dream of reclaiming the Iron Throne.


Years later, Daenerys Targaryen, known as the Mother of Dragons, awakened three dragons—the first seen in over a century—and began her quest to restore the glory of House Targaryen.

🐉DRAGONS🐉

Balerion the Black Dread

Rider: Aegon I Targaryen

Color: Black scales, fiery red eyes

Size: One of the largest dragons in history

Status: Deceased

Balerion was the largest and most powerful dragon of House Targaryen. Known as the Black Dread, his flames were said to be capable of melting stone and steel.

Vhagar

Rider: Visenya Targaryen / Aemond Targaryen

Color: Bronze and brown

Size: One of the largest dragons ever known

Status: Deceased

Vhagar was one of the three dragons used during Aegon's Conquest. Centuries later, she became the mount of Aemond Targaryen during the Dance of the Dragons.

Meraxes

Rider: Rhaenys Targaryen

Color: Silver

Size: Enormous

Status: Deceased

Meraxes was ridden by Queen Rhaenys Targaryen during the conquest of Westeros. She was one of the three legendary dragons that helped establish Targaryen rule.

Caraxes

Rider: Daemon Targaryen

Color: Red

Size: Large and powerful

Status: Deceased

Known as the Blood Wyrm, Caraxes was one of the most dangerous dragons of his time. He was fiercely loyal to Prince Daemon Targaryen.

Syrax

Rider: Rhaenyra Targaryen

Color: Yellow and golden

Size: Large

Status: Deceased

Syrax was the personal dragon of Queen Rhaenyra Targaryen. She was named after a Valyrian goddess and was kept at Dragonstone for much of her life.

Meleys the Red Queen

Rider: Rhaenys Targaryen

Color: Scarlet red

Size: Very large

Status: Deceased

Meleys was known as the Red Queen because of her brilliant scarlet scales. She was one of the fastest dragons alive during the Dance of the Dragons.

Vermax

Rider: Jacaerys Velaryon

Color: Green

Size: Medium

Status: Deceased

Vermax was the dragon of Prince Jacaerys Velaryon. He accompanied his rider during important missions throughout the Dance of the Dragons.

Drogon

Rider: Daenerys Targaryen

Color: Black scales with red wings

Size: Largest of Daenerys' dragons

Status: Alive

Drogon was the largest of Daenerys Targaryen's three dragons. Named after Khal Drogo, he became a powerful symbol of Targaryen restoration.

Rhaegal

Rider: Daenerys Targaryen

Color: Green with bronze wings

Size: Large

Status: Deceased

Rhaegal was named after Rhaegar Targaryen. He was one of Daenerys' three dragons and fought alongside her during her campaign in Westeros.

Viserion

Rider: Daenerys Targaryen

Color: Cream and gold

Size: Large

Status: Deceased

Viserion was named after Viserys Targaryen. He was one of Daenerys' three dragons and later became an undead dragon under the control of the Night King.

Sunfyre

Rider: Aegon II Targaryen

Color: Golden scales

Size: Large

Status: Deceased

Sunfyre was one of the most beautiful dragons in Westerosi history, famous for his brilliant golden scales and golden wings.

Dreamfyre

Rider: Helaena Targaryen

Color: Pale blue with silver markings

Size: Large

Status: Deceased

Dreamfyre was an ancient dragon known for her pale coloring. She was ridden by Princess Helaena Targaryen.

Tessarion

Rider: Daeron Targaryen

Color: Blue with cobalt wings

Size: Medium

Status: Deceased

Known as the Blue Queen, Tessarion was the dragon of Prince Daeron Targaryen during the Dance of the Dragons.

Seasmoke

Rider: Laenor Velaryon

Color: Pale grey

Size: Medium

Status: Alive

Seasmoke was a swift and agile dragon originally ridden by Laenor Velaryon.

Arrax

Rider: Lucerys Velaryon

Color: Pearly white and pale yellow

Size: Young

Status: Deceased

Arrax was the young dragon ridden by Lucerys Velaryon during the Dance of the Dragons.

Tyraxes

Rider: Joffrey Velaryon

Color: Golden and orange

Size: Young

Status: Deceased

Tyraxes was the dragon of Prince Joffrey Velaryon and was still relatively young during the Dance of the Dragons.

Moondancer

Rider: Baela Targaryen

Color: Pale green

Size: Small

Status: Deceased

Moondancer was the swift young dragon ridden by Baela Targaryen during the Dance of the Dragons.

Sheepstealer

Rider: Nettles

Color: Brown

Size: Large

Status: Unknown

Sheepstealer was a wild dragon living around Dragonstone. He earned his name because he preferred sheep as prey.

Cannibal

Rider: None

Color: Black

Size: Enormous

Status: Unknown

Cannibal was one of the wild dragons of Dragonstone. He was feared for attacking other dragons and eating their remains.

Grey Ghost

Rider: None

Color: Pale grey

Size: Medium

Status: Deceased

Grey Ghost was a shy wild dragon that lived on Dragonstone. He avoided humans and was rarely seen.

Silverwing

Rider: Alysanne Targaryen

Color: Silver

Size: Large

Status: Unknown

Silverwing was a beautiful silver dragon ridden by Queen Alysanne Targaryen. She was known for her calm temperament.

Vermithor

Rider: Jaehaerys I Targaryen

Color: Bronze

Size: Enormous

Status: Deceased

Known as the Bronze Fury, Vermithor was one of the largest dragons alive during the Dance of the Dragons.
